Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the DevPocket mobile app ("the app", "we", "us") handles your information. DevPocket is a developer toolkit that lets you browse GitHub repositories, edit and commit files, and trigger GitHub Actions workflows from your phone.

Short version: DevPocket runs entirely on your device. We operate no servers and do not collect, store, or sell your data. Your information is processed only to talk to GitHub on your behalf, using the access you grant during sign-in.

1. Information we access

  • Your GitHub profile and account – When you sign in with GitHub (OAuth device flow), we receive your GitHub username and public profile so we can show you your account in the app.
  • Your repositories and code – The repositories, files, and branches you browse or edit are fetched from GitHub and displayed on your device. Content you choose to commit is sent to GitHub, not to us.
  • Workflows and run logs – The workflows you list, dispatch, and stream are read from and written to GitHub through the GitHub API.
  • On-device session data – Your access token and cached profile are stored locally on your device. See "Storage and security" below.

2. How we use your information

We use the information described above only to provide the app's functionality: authenticating you, listing and editing repositories, and running GitHub Actions workflows. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

We do not host any servers and do not store your data in any cloud database controlled by us. Your GitHub access token is saved in your device's secure storage (the operating system's encrypted keystore, via Expo SecureStore). All communication with GitHub happens over encrypted HTTPS connections.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties. The only recipient of your data is GitHub, and only when you have authorized it through the app's sign-in flow. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

5. Data retention

Because we maintain no servers or databases, there is nothing for us to retain. Data you request or edit is stored by GitHub under your GitHub account and is subject to GitHub's terms and privacy policy. The access token remains on your device until you sign out, revoke access, or uninstall the app.

6. Your choices and account deletion

You can sign out at any time, which removes your access token from the device. To revoke DevPocket's access to your GitHub account, or to delete your account and data, see our account deletion page.

7. Children's privacy

DevPocket is intended for developers and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
